- Vahid Karimipour, Fabio Benatti and Roberto Floreanini, *Quasi inversion of qubit channels*, Physical Review A **101**, 032109 (2020), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.101.032109, arXiv:1909.06118v5. Downloaded primary v5 PDF to `quasi-inversion.pdf`; text extraction to `quasi-inversion.txt`; primary abstract metadata saved to `quasi-abs.html`. Theorem 1 on PDF page 2 states “The quasi-inverse of any qubit channel can always be taken to be a unitary map.” Appendix A, PDF pages 5–6, proves replacement of a recovery by a unital then mixed-unitary recovery. The average input-output fidelity optimized in that paper and maximally mixed entanglement fidelity satisfy F_avg=(2 F_e+1)/3 for qubit CPTP channels, so their optimizers agree. This theorem supplies the unitary-recovery step; absorption into the encoder is then elementary trace cyclicity.
